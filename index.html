Privacy Policy

App: SSC English (com.malik.sscenglish) • Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SSC English Android app ("the app", "we") handles information. SSC English is an English-language exam preparation app for students.

1. Information we do not collect

  • The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number, address or email to use the app.
  • We do not run our own servers that store your personal data.
  • The app does not include any third-party analytics or tracking SDK other than Google AdMob (described below).

2. Data stored on your device

Your study data stays on your own device. This includes bookmarks, mock test history and scores, wrong-answer (Galti) vault, diary notes, favourite words, study files you add, streak and coin progress, and app settings. We cannot see this data. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If you use the in-app backup feature, the backup file is created on your device and shared or stored only where you choose. If Android device backup is turned on, Android/Google may back up app data according to your own device settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Google Advertising ID and similar device information to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings. See Google's information on how it uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional premium plans (ad-free access) are bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play whether an active purchase exists on your Google account so it can unlock premium features.

5. Content downloads

To keep question papers, vocabulary and practice content up to date, the app downloads content files over the internet from a public content repository hosted on GitHub. Like any internet request, the hosting service can see your IP address and basic request information. No personal data from your app is sent in these requests.

6. Notifications

With your permission, the app can show reminder notifications such as Word of the Day and streak reminders. These are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device notification settings.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to download content and show ads.
  • Notifications: to show the reminders described above.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob for ads.
  • Billing: to process optional premium purchases through Google Play.

8. Children

The app is intended for exam aspirants and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share your personal information. Data on your device is stored in the app's private storage. Information handled by Google (AdMob, Google Play) is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

10. Your choices and data deletion

Because your study data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. You can also manage ad personalization and notification permissions in your device settings.

11. Disclaimer

SSC English is an independent educational test preparation app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) or any government organization.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date changed.
